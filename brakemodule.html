LESSON 1 OF 20
Introduction
Get an overview of what you will learn — the complete brake pad replacement process from start to finish.
Estimated time: 5 min
Difficulty: Easy
What You Will Learn
1
Understand why regular brake pad replacements are essential for vehicle safety.
2
Learn the tools and materials required for a safe brake pad change.
3
Follow a 16-step procedure from preparation to completion.
LESSON 2 OF 20
Tools & Materials
Gather all necessary tools and materials before starting the brake pad replacement.
Estimated time: 5 min
Difficulty: Easy
Required Tools
1
Hydraulic jack and jack stands
2
Lug wrench or impact drill
3
New brake pads
4
C-clamp or caliper piston compressor tool
5
Socket set
6
Bungee cord or zip tie
Safety Reminder
Always wear gloves and safety glasses. Brake dust can be harmful.
LESSON 3 OF 20 — STEP 1
Prepare the Vehicle
Park on a flat surface, engage parking brake, block the wheels.
5 min
Easy
Instructions
1
Park on a flat, level surface and engage parking brake.
2
Place wheel chocks behind the rear tires.
Safety Reminder
Never work on a slope, and ensure the vehicle is perfectly stationary.
LESSON 4 OF 20 — STEP 2
Loosen the Lug Nuts
Break the lug nuts loose before raising the vehicle.
5 min
Easy
Instructions
1
Use a lug wrench to perform a quarter-turn counterclockwise on each lug nut.
2
Do NOT remove the nuts completely while the vehicle is on the ground.
LESSON 5 OF 20 — STEP 3
Raise the Vehicle
Use a jack to lift the car and secure it with jack stands.
5 min
Medium
Instructions
1
Position the hydraulic jack on the manufacturer-recommended pinch weld.
2
Lift until the tire clears the ground completely.
3
Place a jack stand under a secure frame rail and lower the car onto it.
LESSON 6 OF 20 — STEP 4
Remove the Wheel
Fully unscrew the lug nuts and take the wheel off.
2 min
Easy
Instructions
1
Completely undo the loosened lug nuts.
2
Pull the wheel straight out.
3
Place the wheel flat under the car frame for extra protection.
LESSON 7 OF 20 — STEP 5
Inspect Rotor & Caliper
Examine the brake rotor and caliper for scoring, wear, or fluid leaks.
3 min
Easy
Instructions
1
Run your finger over the rotor — check for deep groves or pitting.
2
Inspect the caliper boot for brake fluid leaks.
LESSON 8 OF 20 — STEP 6
Remove Caliper Bolts
Unbolt the brake caliper from its mounting bracket.
7 min
Medium
Instructions
1
Locate the two slider pins/bolts on the back of the caliper.
2
Use your socket set to loosen and remove the bolts.
LESSON 9 OF 20 — STEP 7
Secure the Caliper
Hang the caliper out of the way safely.
3 min
Easy
Instructions
1
Slide the caliper off the rotor.
2
Use a bungee cord or a zip tie to hang the caliper securely from the suspension spring.
LESSON 10 OF 20 — STEP 8
Remove the Old Pads
Pop the old brake pads out of the mounting bracket.
3 min
Easy
Instructions
1
Note how the metal retaining clips are placed.
2
Slide old pads straight away from the rotor.
3
Keep one old pad to use for compressing the piston next.
LESSON 11 OF 20 — STEP 9
Inspect Mounting Bracket
Ensure the bracket hardware is in good condition.
2 min
Easy
Instructions
1
Remove old retaining clips.
2
Use a wire brush to clean the bracket.
LESSON 12 OF 20 — STEP 10
Compress the Caliper Piston
Push the caliper's hydraulic piston back into its housing.
5 min
Medium
Instructions
1
Place your old brake pad directly against the piston face.
2
Position a C-clamp so it squeezes the back of the caliper and the old brake pad.
3
Tighten the clamp until the piston is flush with the caliper housing.
LESSON 13 OF 20 — STEP 11
Apply Brake Grease
Lubricate parts to prevent brake squeal.
3 min
Easy
Instructions
1
Apply a small amount of brake caliper grease to the slider pins.
2
Apply grease to the backing plate of the new pads, avoiding the friction material.
LESSON 14 OF 20 — STEP 12
Install the New Brake Pads
Snap the new pads into the bracket.
5 min
Easy
Instructions
1
Snap on the new retaining clips into the bracket.
2
Slide the new pads squarely into the mounting bracket against the rotor.
LESSON 15 OF 20 — STEP 13
Reinstall the Caliper
Swing the caliper back down over the new, thicker brake pads.
5 min
Easy
Instructions
1
Remove the bungee cord / zip tie from the caliper.
2
Slide the caliper over the new pads. Ensure you do not twist the brake hose.
3
Re-insert the slider bolts and tighten snugly with your socket wrench.
LESSON 16 OF 20 — STEP 14
Reinstall the Wheel
Put the wheel back on the vehicle.
3 min
Easy
Instructions
1
Lift the wheel back onto the lug studs.
2
Hand-thread all lug nuts in a star-pattern to hold it securely.
LESSON 17 OF 20 — STEP 15
Lower the Vehicle
Set the car back to the ground securely.
5 min
Medium
Instructions
1
Jack the car up slightly to remove the jack stand.
2
Slowly release jack pressure.
3
Tighten the lug nuts heavily in a star-pattern.
LESSON 18 OF 20 — STEP 16
Pump the Brakes
Rebuild brake pressure before shifting out of Park.
2 min
Easy
Instructions
1
Sit in the driver's seat.
2
Pump the brake pedal firmly 4-5 times until it feels very stiff.
Safety Reminder
DO NOT drive without pumping the brakes first.

LESSON 20 OF 20
Summary
Review the key takeaways from the Brake Pad Replacement module.
Key Takeaways
1
Always secure the vehicle and never rely on a hydraulic jack alone.
2
Never stretch or dangle the brake lines.
3
Compressing the piston is essential to fit new, thicker pads.
4
Always pump the brake pedal several times to restore hydraulic pressure before driving.
